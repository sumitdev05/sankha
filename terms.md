## Using Sankha

- Members can set one entrance song per server with `/setentry`, using a YouTube link. Server owners and entry admins
  can also upload audio files and manage other members' entrances.
- Clips are at most 15 seconds long, and source videos at most 10 minutes. Other limits may change at any time.
- Server owners decide who their entry admins are and can remove any entrance in their server.
